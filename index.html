AI SALES COMPANY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the individual eBay seller using the application name AI SALES COMPANY collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information obtained through authorized eBay APIs.

1. Information We Process

Depending on an order and the permissions granted by eBay, information may include a buyer's eBay user identifier, name, shipping address, contact details provided by eBay, order number, purchased products, quantities, order status, and shipping or tracking information.

We do not intentionally collect payment card details through this application.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information only when needed to:

  • Receive and manage orders placed through eBay.
  • Arrange product fulfillment and delivery.
  • Provide shipping and tracking updates.
  • Handle customer service, returns, refunds, and disputes.
  • Meet applicable legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

Where necessary to fulfill an order, we may share relevant information, such as the recipient's name and delivery address, with the product supplier, fulfillment provider, and shipping carrier responsible for that order.

We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant fulfillment, delivery, or support purpose. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, provide customer support, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal or tax obligations. Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized, subject to mandatory legal retention requirements.

5. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

A buyer may contact the eBay seller through eBay Messages to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held in connection with an order.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and any mandatory record-retention requirements. Where eBay sends an account deletion notification, the seller will identify the relevant information and handle the request in accordance with eBay requirements and applicable law.

6. Data Security

We use access controls and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Access is limited to what is needed for order operations.

7. Third-Party Services

Orders are placed through eBay. Fulfillment and delivery may involve independent suppliers, fulfillment providers, and shipping carriers. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy requests relating to an order, contact the individual seller through the eBay Messages service associated with the transaction.

9. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our data practices or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
